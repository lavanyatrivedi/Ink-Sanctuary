Build a single-page website for Order Tattoo, a quiet, minimalist tattoo studio owned by Manpreet Kaur. Core feeling: scrolling through this site should feel like a calm walk — not a slideshow. Use scroll-triggered reveal animations (fade-up, subtle parallax, text tracing in like ink spreading, images unmasking slowly) so each section builds on the last. Hero — Just "Order Tattoo" and a one-line quiet tagline (something like "Ink, intention, and everything in between"). Minimal, full-screen, calm entrance animation.

Why & When to Get One — thoughtful, non-pushy section on reasons people tattoo (memory, identity, transformation, grief, celebration, belonging) and practical readiness cues (permanence, healing time, choosing it for yourself not a trend, aftercare commitment). Keep tone reflective, not salesy.
Studio / Manpreet's Philosophy — short section on Manpreet Kaur's approach — calm, intentional, collaborative tattooing. Space for a portrait-style stock image.
Contact — Instagram handle, phone number, email — laid out cleanly, minimal icons, maybe a closing line like "Tell us what you want to carry with you."

Images: add high-quality, calm/aesthetic stock tattoo studio images wherever visually needed (hero background, history section accents, studio/portrait section) — muted tones matching the palette, nothing gory or flashy.
